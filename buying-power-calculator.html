Let's Get You Ready to Buy

With a score below 580, most lenders — including FHA — won't approve a mortgage today. But with the right steps, most buyers can qualify within 6–12 months.

What moves the needle fastest

💳Pay down card balances — keeping utilization below 30% can add 40–100 points quickly.
✅Never miss a payment — 35% of your score. Set up autopay on everything.
🚫Don't open new accounts — each hard inquiry lowers your score temporarily.
📋Dispute errors on your report — 1 in 5 reports has an error that hurts the score.
📈Become an authorized user — get added to an older, low-balance card for a fast boost.
